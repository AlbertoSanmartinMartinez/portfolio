# Intent: Portfolio site

## Problem

People who want to know Alberto's work only find his LinkedIn profile today. He doesn't control how it looks, it doesn't show his projects in depth, and it is not his own place to start a conversation.

## Users

- Potential clients evaluating him for AI and agent projects.
- Recruiters and companies looking at his experience.
- People from the technical community interested in his projects.

They arrive from a link (LinkedIn, GitHub, an email signature) and read on a phone or a desktop, in Spanish or English.

## Outcome

A visitor understands in under a minute who Alberto is, what he has done and how to reach him, in Spanish or English. The content comes from his LinkedIn export and is easy to update. No metric yet: it is decided after launch.

## Out of scope

- Blog.
- Visit analytics.
- Downloadable PDF CV.
- A private panel to read contact messages.
- The contact form itself (`specs/002`) and the deployment (`specs/003`).
