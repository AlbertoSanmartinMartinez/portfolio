# Intent: Contact form

## Problem

Today a visitor who wants to talk to Alberto has to copy an email address or go to LinkedIn. Some leave instead, and Alberto has no single place where those first messages arrive.

## Users

The same visitors as `specs/001`: potential clients, recruiters and companies, and people from the technical community, writing in Spanish or English from a phone or a desktop.

## Outcome

A visitor sends a message from the Contact section in under a minute, sees that it arrived, and Alberto gets it in his inbox right away. Messages are kept safely for 12 months and then deleted. No metric yet, as for `specs/001`.

## Out of scope

- A panel to read or answer messages: Alberto answers from his email.
- A copy or auto-reply to the sender.
- Third-party anti-spam scripts or cookies.
- Deployment (`specs/003`).

## Open questions

- [ ] The email provider and the sending domain, decided in the plan.
- [ ] Privacy information: a short notice under the form plus a privacy page in both languages (recommended; Alberto to confirm at the spec gate).
